## Safe Saving Practices

Here are the rules on "safe saving" in a nutshell:

1. Don't save scum (i.e., don't continuously and repeatedly save and load the game in order to pickpocket someone successfully).

2. Don't pick up quest items that are placed in the game world, then save and quit your game (items in containers are fine).

That's it. Those are the safe saving rules. Every other rule you may have been told has been specifically designed to blame you for any problems you encounter. And honestly, these two things won't even break your save, it'll only break that specific quest and/or NPC.
